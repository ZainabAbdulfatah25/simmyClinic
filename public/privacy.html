Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

SimmyClinic ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our patients, medical practitioners, and visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access or use the SimmyClinic web platform at https://simmyclinic.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, schedule medical consultations, order pharmaceuticals, or authenticate using Google Sign-In, we collect:

  • Google Account Information: When you choose to authenticate via Google OAuth, we receive your basic profile information (Full Name, verified Email Address, and Profile Avatar) to create and authenticate your SimmyClinic account.
  • Contact Information: Phone number, residential or delivery address for courier logistics.
  • Clinical & Medical Data: Symptoms, consultation notes, laboratory test orders, and prescription requests shared with your healthcare provider.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for legitimate clinical, logistical, and administrative healthcare operations:

  • To authenticate and manage your secure login via email or Google OAuth.
  • To connect patients with certified doctors and medical specialists for telehealth consultations.
  • To process lab diagnostics requests and deliver pharmacy prescriptions.
  • To send critical medical notifications, appointment reminders, and booking receipts.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Protection

We do NOT sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal or medical data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

Data is shared solely with certified healthcare personnel (licensed doctors, verified pharmacists, and logistics partners) directly involved in your medical care.

4. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive health and user profile data is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) and protected at rest on secure cloud infrastructure with enterprise-grade row-level security access controls.

5. User Rights, Data Access & Deletion

You have full rights to inspect, update, or request the permanent deletion of your account and personal data at any time. To request account deletion or data export, contact our data protection team at support@simmyclinic.com or abdulfatahzainab48@gmail.com.
